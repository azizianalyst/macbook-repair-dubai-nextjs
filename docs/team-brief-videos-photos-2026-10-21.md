# Repair team brief: videos and photos

**MacBook Repair Dubai · 21 October 2026**

**Why we're doing this:** Google shows videos and photos above the normal results. For "MacBook screen replacement" there is a row of YouTube videos, and none of them are ours. For "MacBook repair Dubai" there is a row of photos, and none of them are ours either. Real videos and photos from our own bench are the one thing competitors can't copy.

---

## Part 1: The rules (read before filming)

1. **Real only.** Film and photograph our own bench, our own hands, our own workshop. No stock footage, no AI images, no filters.
2. **Protect the customer.**
   - Never show customer names, phone numbers, emails, job cards or WhatsApp chats.
   - Never show the screen of a customer's device with their files or photos open.
   - Never show customer faces unless the customer agreed in writing.
3. **Say only what is true.** On camera, do **not** say:
   - "Apple authorised" or "Apple certified" (we are independent)
   - "guaranteed"
   - "best in Dubai"
   - "No. 1"
   - any price in AED (prices are being decided separately)
   - any time promise we don't always keep ("30 minutes", "same day")

   Say what we actually do and what you actually see.
4. **Do not show:**
   - The address, phone number or opening hours on camera. We add those in the description so they're always correct.
   - Any other company's logo or brand, except the Apple device itself.

## Part 2: The videos

### How to film (every video)

- **Shape:** horizontal (landscape) 16:9, 4K or 1080p, 30 fps. Google's video row shows horizontal YouTube videos.
- **Vertical version:** afterwards, a 30–60 second vertical clip for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram. Office will cut it from the same footage, so you don't film twice.
- **Camera:**
  - Phone or camera on a tripod or an overhead arm. No handheld shaking.
  - Overhead (top-down) for the repair itself, plus one side angle.
- **Light:** bright, even light on the bench mat. No harsh shadows.
- **Sound:** clip-on microphone on the technician. Turn off music and the AC noise if you can.
- **Length:** 3–8 minutes for the main video. Keep only the real steps; cut waiting time.

### Structure (every video)

1. **First 10 seconds:** show the fault, and say the problem in the words customers search, e.g. "This MacBook Air has horizontal lines on the screen."
2. **The device:** model and year, e.g. "MacBook Pro 14-inch, M3, 2023". Read it from the bottom case or About This Mac, not the customer's screen.
3. **What causes it:** in one or two plain sentences, from your bench experience. Dubai heat, sand, humidity and AC condensation are real causes; mention them only where they apply.
4. **The repair:** the actual steps, filmed close.
5. **The test at the end:** the device working.
6. **The end:** "We're MacBook Repair Dubai in Dubai Media City. Message us on WhatsApp if your Mac has the same problem." No address or phone number on screen; we add them in the description.

### What to send to the office

- the raw video files
- **3 photos from the same job** (see Part 3)
- one line of notes: device model, fault, what fixed it, how long the repair took

## Part 3: The photos

### How to shoot (every photo)

- **Camera:** phone or camera at full quality. Horizontal, at least 1600 pixels wide (any modern phone is fine).
- **Light:** natural or bright even light. No flash glare on screens.
- **Real only:** no filters, no text on the photo, no logos added, no AI editing. Crop and brighten only.
- **Clean and tidy:** a clean bench mat, tools laid out, no clutter, no food or drinks.
- **No customer data on any screen** (same rule as videos).

### Photos for each repair (3 per repair)

For **each** of these repairs, take 3 photos during a real job:

1. **The fault:** the device on our bench showing the problem (cracked screen, lines, swollen battery, corrosion, stuck keys).
2. **Mid-repair:** hands and tools at work, the device open, the part visible.
3. **The result:** the repaired device working, on the bench.

The repairs, one set each:

- **MacBook:**
  - screen
  - horizontal lines / display cable
  - battery
  - swollen battery
  - keyboard
  - trackpad
  - logic board (microscope shot)
  - water damage (corrosion close-up)
  - not turning on (meter or diagnosis)
  - overheating (dusty fan)
  - charging port
  - data recovery (drive or board, no customer files)
  - Flexgate
- **iMac:** screen or internals
- **iPad:** screen, battery, water damage
- **iPhone:** screen, battery, back glass, charging port, water damage

That's 25 repairs × 3 = **75 photos**. Spread them over the jobs you do anyway; don't stage fake repairs.
