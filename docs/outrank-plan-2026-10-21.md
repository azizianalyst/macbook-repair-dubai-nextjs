Nobody can promise 10 times a competitor's rankings. What we *can* do is be far ahead on the things Google rewards and competitors don't do:
- 20+ real repair videos
- 75+ real bench photos
- steady new reviews that name the repair
- clean links earned on merit

## The five pillars

### 1. Map pack (the biggest gap)

Why we lose: fewer reviews than the #1 listing, the shop is about 20 km from central Dubai, and our listing doesn't list any repair services.

- **Reviews (team, every day).** Ask every finished customer: a QR code at the counter, plus a WhatsApp link within 24 hours. Ask them to name the repair. We need about 20 a month to pass 310 by January. Reply to every review. (War Room t16, urgent)
- **Business Profile services (owner, this week).** Add each MacBook repair as a service with a short description: screen, battery, keyboard, logic board, liquid damage, data recovery, charging port, overheating. (t-rank-gbp-services)
- **Business Profile activity (office, weekly).**
  - the 3 best new bench photos
  - every new video
  - one post about a real repair from that week
  - answers in the Q&A section
- **Same details everywhere.** Fix the conflicting name/address/phone on macbookrepair.ae, the Facebook page and azizitechnologies.ae. (t-nap-conflicts)
- **Area tracking.** A map-grid rank tracker around Media City (Semrush Map Rank Tracker once access is renewed, or BrightLocal). This is the only way to see positions area by area. (t18)

### 2. Authority (links and trust)

- **Decide D3:** azizitechnologies.ae competes with us on 16 of 23 searches. Consolidate or split cleanly; until then link work is split between two sites.
- **Decide D14:** spam links. Never buy links again.
- **Citations, 10 a month (owner sends, Claude drafts):**
  - UAE directories with identical name, address and phone

  - the 6 drafts already in automation/drafts/

  (t17, t-send-outreach)
- **Earned links (Claude finds and drafts, owner sends).** From the 500 sites that link to competitors but not us, keep the genuine UAE and tech sites and request a listing or mention. (t-rank-backlink-gap)
- **Our own data as a story.** A short "Dubai summer and your MacBook" report built from our real repair logs (battery swelling, fan failures, liquid damage by month), offered to UAE tech and lifestyle sites. Only numbers from our own records. This is the kind of link competitors can't copy.

### 4. Videos and photos

The topics are in the team brief: 21 videos and 75 photos, all mapped to searches we already get. When a video arrives, Claude:
1. writes the YouTube title, description (with address, phone and hours from the fact sheet), chapters and tags
2. cuts the 30–60 second vertical version (Remotion project) for Shorts, TikTok and Instagram
3. embeds the video on the matching page, with VideoObject markup and a short written summary under it
4. adds the 3 job photos to that page with descriptive file names, alt text and captions, and lists them in the image sitemap
5. posts the video and photos to the Business Profile (owner approves)
