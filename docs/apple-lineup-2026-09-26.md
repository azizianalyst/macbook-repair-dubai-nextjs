### 4b. Teardowns and repairability

**iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max: iFixit gives a provisional 7/10.**
- The battery tray is held by 13 screws, with no glue.
- The camera modules are removable.
- The display is still the entry point. The white plastic display frame broke or lifted on 3 of 4 teardown units, which risks the IP68 seal.
- The new 6-blade variable-aperture camera is not repairable at component level. A stuck aperture means replacing the whole main camera assembly.
- Face ID's infrared camera now sits under the display.
- The USB-C port needs a lot of disassembly to replace.
- Sources: https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/20/ifixit-tears-down-iphone-18-pro-explores-variable-aperture-finds-concerning-repair-issue/ ; https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/09/21/sensitive-camera-blades-and-thermal-chainmail-surface-in-iphone-18-pro-teardown ; https://www.theregister.com/personal-tech/2026/09/21/skip-the-upgrade-iphone-18-pro-aperture-about-as-unrepairable-as-it-gets-ifixit-finds/5297970

**iPhone 18 Pro materials:**
- Aluminium unibody. It is **not titanium**.
- Ceramic Shield 2 on the front.
- A **Ceramic Shield back** panel (the MagSafe cut-out), colour-matched to the frame.
- Sources: https://www.apple.com/ae/iphone-18-pro/specs/ ; https://www.macobserver.com/news/iphone-18-pro-aluminum-not-titanium-2026-iphone-frame-materials/

**iPhone Duo:**
- Titanium frame and hinge cover.
- Ceramic Shield 2 front and Ceramic Shield back.
- The inner display has a nano-textured polymer layer.
- **No real teardown exists yet**, because the phone ships on 23 Oct. iFixit's 9 Sep piece is a pre-launch opinion ("we're skeptical"). It raises concerns about hinge dust, inner-screen replacement effort and glued batteries.
- A "2/10" score seen in one search summary could **not** be verified, so treat it as false.
- Sources: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/ ; https://www.ifixit.com/News/119205/will-the-iphone-duo-be-repairable-were-skeptical

**MacBook Neo: iFixit 6/10**, the "most repairable MacBook in 14 years".
- The battery sits in a tray held by 18 screws.
- The display, trackpad, battery and Touch ID can be swapped without parts-pairing warnings.
- The keyboard can be replaced, but it takes 41 screws.
- RAM (8GB) and storage are soldered, and it uses pentalobe screws.
- Sources: https://www.ifixit.com/News/116152/macbook-neo-is-the-most-repairable-macbook-in-14-years ; https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/03/13/ifixit-macbook-neo-teardown-confirms-that-it-is-apples-most-repairable-laptop-since-2012

**14" MacBook Pro M5: iFixit 4/10.**
- Battery replacement means removing nearly every component, which iFixit calls "absurdly complex".
- Small gain: the trackpad no longer has to come out to reach the battery pull tabs.
- Apple published repair guides on day one.
- Sources: https://9to5mac.com/2025/10/24/ifixit-tears-down-the-m5-macbook-pro-and-finds-small-but-welcome-repairability-improvements/ ; https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/24/ifixit-tears-down-m5-macbook-pro/

**MacBook Air M5:**
- Pentalobe screws on the outside.
- The battery uses stretch-release adhesive plus screws, and the logic board must come out to reach it.
- RAM and SSD are soldered.
- Source: https://www.ifixit.com/Device/MacBook_Air_13%22_2026_%28M5%29

### 4c. Common early faults reported

**iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max (September 2026):**
- Reports of freezes and reboots after a failed Face ID attempt, especially when unlocking locked apps.
- Reports of touchscreen freezes.
