**How to use it:**
- One topic is one URL. Every keyword under a topic belongs on that one page, as H2s, FAQs and body copy. Never build a second page for the same topic, because that is what causes cannibalisation.
- **Optimise / Strengthen** means the page already exists; improve it rather than creating a new one.
- **NEW** means no page targets the topic yet; the suggested slug follows the site's `keyword = slug` rule.
- **Pos** is our current Google position for the topic's main keyword (Semrush, 25 Sep 2026), where we have one.
- Before writing, follow the rules in `TRUST-CLAIMS.md` and `automation/AUTOPILOT.md`:
  - no AED prices (quotes go through WhatsApp)
  - never "Apple Authorised"
  - warranty tiers from `site.ts`
  - 40–60-word answer block
  - 4–6 FAQs
  - at least 3 inbound links
  - WhatsApp as the main CTA
