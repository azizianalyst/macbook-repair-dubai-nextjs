## Defects

### 🔴 Critical

1. **"certified Apple specialists" in the GBP description.**
   The business is **not** an Apple Authorized Service Provider — and the website says so explicitly in at least 6 places (`src/components/blocks/VsAppleStore.tsx:79`, `src/views/Warranty.tsx:45`, and others). The GBP contradicts the site's own disclaimer. This is an Apple trademark-misrepresentation risk and a GBP content-policy risk. Flagged in the June audit; **still live**.

2. ~~Services field completely empty.~~ **CORRECTED 2026-09-01: this finding was wrong.**
   Live-checked in Business Profile Manager: the Services field has entries — a mix of legitimate repair services, off-strategy software/accessories listings, and keyword-stuffed near-duplicates. It is not empty, it is *unmanaged*: broad and diluted rather than the granular, intent-matched 103-item list in Module 3. Because publishing is a **replace, not a merge** (`updateServices()`), the 103-service publish is destructive to whatever is live now — check the `/admin/gbp` dry-run count before publishing, don't assume a blank slate.

3. **Site AggregateRating is stale and understates the profile.**
   Live JSON-LD emits `"reviewCount":"216"` / `"ratingValue":"5"` (`src/content/business.generated.ts:20`); GBP shows **232**. Schema that disagrees with the corroborating source is a trust/mismatch signal. (Understated, not inflated — so no penalty exposure, but it must track.)

4. **Price-policy conflict is unresolved and live.**
   Branch `hide-prices-whatsapp` (HEAD `a5331b2`, "wip: … views pending") strips prices site-wide, but **the live site still shows them** (AED 150 / 299 / 1,000 / 2,449 …) and still emits `"priceRange":"AED 150 - AED 3,500"`. Meanwhile `src/content/blog-taxonomy.generated.ts:12` promises *"No call-for-price games: we publish the numbers"* — the exact opposite of the migration. **This decides what Modules 3 and 4 may contain and must be settled before writing services/products.**

### 🟠 Important

5. **Brand/entity collision with a different business.**
   `macbookrepairsdubai.ae` (Regal Tower, Business Bay, +971 56 970 9325) trades as **"Macbook Repair Dubai"**, ranks directly beneath you for your own brand name, and owns the **LinkedIn "MacBook Repair Dubai"** profile (which points at *their* domain). Not fixable by them-removal; must be out-competed on entity strength and claimed social surfaces.

6. **Own listings compete with each other.**
   Google's "people also search for" lists **Azizi Technologies (4.9 ★, 643 reviews)** — your own second listing — as a competitor to this one. Three Azizi-family GBPs exist in the same account (MacBook Repair Dubai, Azizi Technologies × 2). Entity strategy needed, not accidental cannibalisation.

7. **Site self-contradiction on volume repaired.**
   Homepage title: "40,000+ Fixed". About page title: **"5,000+ Repairs"** (`src/lib/page-meta-overrides.generated.ts:295`). Both live. Pick one true number.

8. **Post cadence ~3 weeks, not weekly.** Latest 6 days ago, previous Aug 8. Freshness is a map-pack input and the June Maps decline tracked posting gaps.

9. **Address string drift.** GBP "Al Safouh Second" vs site "Al Sufouh". Minor but it is a NAP token mismatch.

10. **Description claims "200+ five-star reviews"** — understates 232, is promotional (GBP discourages promotional language in descriptions), and goes stale by design.

### 🟡 Opportunity

11. Q&A empty — crowd-editable and will eventually be filled by someone else.
12. Photos not categorised; no interior/exterior/team/process sets. Process shots are the strongest untapped differentiator.
13. Secondary categories not verified in this pass (June: 8 set, incl. "Used computer store" which dilutes repair focus; **iPhone/iPad repair categories still absent**).

## Binding constraint

> **CORRECTED 2026-09-01:** the "completeness is binding" framing below rested on "Services is empty," which live-checking in Business Profile Manager disproved (see Defect 2, corrected above). Services isn't empty, it's unmanaged — diluted, off-strategy, keyword-stuffed. That's a real and fixable relevance gap, just a different mechanism than "nothing for Google to match against."

> **Completeness is *still* the more fixable lever, restated accurately:** Reviews (232 @ 5.0) already beat every genuine third-party rival in the set — that lever has less headroom short-term. Products and Q&A genuinely are empty. Services needs *replacing* (granular, intent-matched, category-substantiated) rather than *filling*. Modules 2–5 are still worth more this quarter than review volume, but Module 3 is a swap-out, not a first fill — treat the live count as a real, populated field you're overwriting, not a blank one.

This still inverts the skill's usual default (reviews-binding). Review velocity still matters for the long-run gap to GEEKS, but it is **not** what is capping current visibility.
