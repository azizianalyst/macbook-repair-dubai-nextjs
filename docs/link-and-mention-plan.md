# Backlinks & Brand Mentions Plan (#4) — off-site, ongoing

> Why this matters most for ranking + AI: for AI search, **brand mentions correlate ~3× more with
> visibility (r≈0.66) than backlinks (r≈0.22)**. ~68% of LLM citations are third-party (Reddit ~22%,
> Wikipedia ~19–48%, review sites ~17%, YouTube ~10%). For classic SERPs, referring domains still
> drive rankings. So we pursue **links AND mentions** together. None of this is code — it's outreach/PR.

## A "good" link (qualify before spending effort) — target ≥5/7
DR ≥30 · site traffic ≥1,000/mo · topically/locally relevant · dofollow · in main editorial content ·
real multi-keyword site (not a PBN) · verified contact email.
Anchors: keep branded 30–50%, exact-match ("macbook repair dubai") **under 10%**.

## Phase 1 — Quick wins (weeks 1–4)
1. **Unlinked mention reclamation** (easiest). Google-alert / search `"MacBook Repair Dubai"` and
   `"Azizi Technologies"` minus our domain → ask sites that name us to add a link. The mention alone
   already helps AI; the link helps SERPs.
2. **Local citations** (also feeds local pack + AI "near me") — see `docs/local-seo-actions.md`:
   Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, Foursquare, UAE directories (Yallalocal, Connect.ae). Use the exact
   canonical NAP. Consistency across sources is itself an AI-trust signal.
3. **Competitor link-gap** — run Link Intersect on the top 3–5 Dubai Apple-repair sites that outrank us;
   pursue every domain linking to 2+ of them first. Highest-ROI prospecting move.

## Phase 2 — Brand mentions on AI-trusted platforms (the AEO flagship)
4. **Reddit** (r/dubai, r/macbook, r/applehelp) — genuinely helpful answers about Dubai repair
   options/costs; not spam. Reddit is ~22% of LLM citations.
5. **YouTube** — short real repair tutorials/teardowns from the workshop (YouTube mentions correlate
   ~0.737 with AI visibility — the single highest off-site signal). Channel already exists (@AziziTechnologies).
6. **"Best MacBook/laptop repair in Dubai" listicles** — get added to roundups (Time Out Dubai,
   local blogs, expat guides). These get cited heavily by ChatGPT/Perplexity for recommendation queries.
7. **Review platforms** — steady Google + Trustpilot reviews (recency feeds AI summaries).

## Phase 3 — Digital PR (highest authority + AI citations)
8. **Original data study** — you have a 21-year repair logbook. Publish a "State of Apple Repair in
   Dubai 2026": most common faults by model, water-damage recovery rates by hour, repair-vs-Apple cost
   deltas. Original stats earn links AND get quoted by AI (stats +22%, quotes +37% citations). Pitch to
   UAE tech/business media (Khaleej Times, Gulf News tech, local tech blogs).
9. **HARO / Qwoted / Help a B2B Writer** — answer journalist queries as the Apple-repair expert
   (Abdul Aziz) → high-authority links + named-expert mentions.
10. **Entity/knowledge-graph** — keep name/category/one-line description identical across site, GBP,
    LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Facebook, YouTube; Organization schema `sameAs` already lists socials.
    Pursue a Wikidata entry if notability allows.

## Outreach templates

> https://macbook-repair-dubai.ae so readers can reach us? Appreciate it — [Name]

**Broken link / resource →**
> Subject: Broken link on [Page]
> Hi [Name], the link to [dead resource] on [URL] 404s. We have an up-to-date guide on [topic]:
> [our URL] — might be a useful replacement. Either way, great resource. — [Name]

## What to AVOID (these get AI sites penalized)
PBNs · paid dofollow links · bulk/spam directories · comment/forum spam · exact-match anchor spikes ·
reciprocal-link schemes · sudden link-velocity spikes.

## KPIs
Referring domains (not total links) · dofollow % · branded-mention count across web · AI Share of Voice
(ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini citation rate) · local citation accuracy % · review velocity.
